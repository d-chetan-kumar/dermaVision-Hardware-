5. SCIENTIFIC OBSERVATIONS & STRATIFICATION LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Perfect 70.00% Training Partition:
   The training partition contains exactly 8,712 images (70.00% of 12,445).
   Validation contains 1,875 images (15.07%), and Testing contains 1,858 images (14.93%).
2. Group Constraints on Exact Class Ratios:
   Because entire multi-image lesions (some comprising up to 31 images) must be assigned
   as indivisible units to a single partition, exact continuous stratification is
   discrete. Despite this constraint, class representation in Validation (BCC: 14.35%,
   Melanoma: 15.63%, Nevus: 15.02%) and Testing (BCC: 15.44%, Melanoma: 14.57%, Nevus: 14.91%)
   deviates by less than 0.65% from the nominal 15.00% target.
3. Missing Lesion ID Independence:
   The 1,886 images lacking lesion_id metadata (BCC: 0, Melanoma: 337, Nevus: 1,549) have
   been assigned unique group IDs ('unk_<image_id>'). While this follows standard ISIC
   benchmark protocol, it cannot biologically guarantee that two unannotated images do
   not originate from the same patient. This limitation must be stated transparently.
